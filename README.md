# PixelPod: Classic Retro Music Player

Bring back the golden age of portable music right to your Android device! PixelPod combines the iconic, nostalgic design of classic click-wheel media players with your modern local music library, giving you a tactile and authentic retro listening experience.

# Key Features

Authentic Click-Wheel Navigation: Relive the satisfying feel of browsing your tracks with a fully interactive, retro-styled interface complete with classic click-wheel mechanics.

Classic Monochrome & Pixel Aesthetic: Immerse yourself in a nostalgic pixel-art display modeled after legendary media players of the 2000s.

Organized Library Access: Easily browse your local collection by Playlists, Artists, Albums, Songs, Recently Added, and Folders.

Seamless Local Playback: Instantly scan, load, and play your device's offline music files without clutter.

Lightweight & Battery Friendly: Designed to be fast, responsive, and easy on your phone's battery while keeping the vintage vibes alive.

# Rediscover Your Music Collection

Whether you're feeling nostalgic for classic hardware or just want a distraction-free, stylish way to enjoy your offline tunes, PixelPod turns your smartphone into a pocket-sized tribute to music history.
